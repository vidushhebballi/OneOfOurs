Questions

Good to know.

Which pouch size should I choose?

100 g pouches work well as a topper or treat. 300 g and 500 g pouches suit a meal for smaller and medium-sized pets, and 1 kg is for big appetites. A full feeding guide by weight is coming soon.

How does bundle pricing work?

The more pouches you buy in one go, the less each pouch costs. Pick 5, 10, 20, 30 or 60 pouches on any recipe page and you’ll see the price per pouch update.

Where do you deliver?

We’re starting in Bengaluru and will expand to more cities soon.

Can I subscribe?

Not yet. Subscriptions are coming soon. For now, simply re-order whenever you’re running low.

How do I switch my pet to One of Ours?

Mix a little of our food into their current food and increase it gradually over about a week. If your pet has a health condition, check with your vet first.
